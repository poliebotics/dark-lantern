## What is published, and what is not

On 8 September 2026 the principal decided to publish the recording's inputs to these proofs. The 112 raw sensor frames of
rows 600 to 711 (24,472,000 bytes each) are served from the data layer under `frames/` (`FRAMES.md`: per frame the
BLAKE3 the chain log and the statements carry and the SHA-256 the receipts carry), and the August camera-derived tensors
(the 4 x 96 x 112 whole-frame reductions `C_int`, the noised frames `Ct_int`, the per-row oracle input arrays
`oracle/final/august_inputs/row_NNNNNN.npz`, the cached August `C_*.npy` rows, the August vector sets with their layer
activations and the exported August oracle sets) are published on the data layer at their package-relative paths
(`LARGE_FILES.md`), or in `source/blobs/final_int16/` where they are small. With them every residual sum can be recomputed
(`capsule/reexecute/`, `VERIFY.md` sections 2a, 6 and 7) and every proof re-made by a holder of the GPU stack. The earlier
revision of this publication held the frames and the tensors pending that decision; the calibration disclosure
(`CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md`) is unchanged by it. The raw frames of rows 0 to 599 (training rows) and the two earlier sessions'
frames are not part of this package. `noise_proposal/` is omitted as a byte-identical duplicate of `source/noise_august/`
(its MANIFEST.json records the comparison). Build outputs under `target/` are omitted; the reproducible build recreates
them. `NODE_PROVE_READY.md`, the node preparation record that `source/FULL_GUEST.md` names as a companion, is not
published: it describes the rented node's address, login, filesystem layout and backups; the toolchain facts it
established (versions, download sources, digests) are reproduced in `VERIFY.md` section 2 and `PINS.json`.

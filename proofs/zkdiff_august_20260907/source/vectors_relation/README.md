`august/noise_proposal/` (not in MANIFEST.json; its own `MANIFEST.json`) holds the proposed noise set for the August proof
rows 600..711, since adopted as the normative August noise rule (`oracle/final/README_FINAL.md` section 5): `noise_{r:06d}.i16`, the G1 CUDA-Philox emulation with seed 20260823 and draw index `r - 600`, quantised
`rint(x * 2^12)`. The frozen evaluator has no August noise branch; this proposal, written before the rule was settled, is what was adopted,
and the batch driver takes the set through `--noise-dir` (published as `noise_august/`).

Raw frames are not stored: `synthetic:a` and `synthetic:b` are splitmix64 streams (`out_i = mix(seed + (i+1)·0x9E3779B97F4A7C15)`,
little-endian words; frame b additionally maps `x < 1000` to `byte == 255` and `1000 <= x < 2000` to `byte & 3`) that the
Rust side regenerates and checks by sha256 (`notes.synthetic_frame_*`).

What the notes record: torch `area` float32 output equals sequential float32 accumulation then `/kh/kw` (0 mismatches on
both frames); the exact rational mean cast to float32 differs from torch's in 38,934 (frame a) and 34,173 (frame b) of
43,008 positions; the pipeline `C_int` (Q12 through the float16 and bfloat16 casts) differs from the rational `C_int` in
39,077 and 24,962 positions; frame b has 7,313 float16-subnormal means and 751 zero means; all 711 August chain
advances reproduce and the last one yields `S_N`.

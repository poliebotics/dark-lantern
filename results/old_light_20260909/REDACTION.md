# Redaction and holds in this publication

Every file here is a copy of a file the three desks wrote on 9 September 2026 or a document the release desk wrote for
this package. The binary results (per-row score arrays, cell-correlation maps, geometry, the PNG figures and sheets) are
byte-identical to the desks' files. Every copied **text** record (the reports, the code, the run scripts, the evaluation and
metrics JSON, the logs, the audit texts) passed through one redaction rule, the redaction rule of the zkdiff August
package extended with this package's path map (`redact_ol.py` in the release desk's staging tree, whose rules this section
summarises): the desks' working directories on the development machine and the rented machines, the development machine's
local data disk and the data-look desk's directory read as package-relative locators where a public object corresponds
(`results/`, `kit/`, `armi/checkpoints/` and the other data-layer paths, `dark-lantern/...` for files published elsewhere
in the Dark Lantern repository) and as bracketed placeholders where none does (`[private working directory]`,
`[preprocessed-row cache]`, `[archive download directory]`, `[local disk work area]`, `[local grid cache]`,
`[private local data]`, `[the desk's run log, on-box]`, `[a separate desk measurement, not published]`); the rented machines
read as `training-box` or `the rented training machine`, the development machine as `the development machine`, provider
instance identifiers as `[instance id]`, the operator's handle as `the principal`, the participant's name, where a
report used it, as `the participant`, and the desk's working name for the 6 September result as its published title, *No Training
Required* (`dark-lantern/results/train_free_coupling_20260906/`). A JSON record is re-parsed after redaction and the staging script refuses unless only
path-like strings changed; every record is then checked for residue (home and node paths, the local data disk, private
addresses, desk-document names, machine names, the handle) and the build refuses on any hit.

## Files whose published bytes differ from the desks' bytes

268 published files differ from their originals through the rule above or the substitutions listed in the next section;
`REDACTION_LEDGER.tsv` lists each with the private and the published SHA-256 and the reason. 14 of them are the
checkpoint derivatives on the data layer: the as-trained `latest.pt` with the machine-path strings in its recorded
training arguments replaced (`args.c_cache_fallback`, `args.cache_dir`, `args.ckpt`, `args.init_from`, `args.old_root`, `args.out`), every model tensor, the optimiser state, the RNG states, the step, the
sampler and every other argument verified equal to the as-trained file by the staging script before the derivative was
written; `PINS.json` carries both digests. The as-run digest lists of the two kits (`KIT_SHA256SUMS_as_run.txt`) name the
private bytes and therefore do not validate the published copies; `SHA256SUMS` does.

Two records are reduced rather than only redacted. The published `pix2pixhd_old_light/ci_summary.json`, and its data-layer copy,
carry 73 of the source's 77 interval groups: the four `results/2024/d2025_*` groups, the January 2025 discriminator's re-scoring,
are removed at staging, and the other 73 groups are unchanged (the kit's `ci_summary.py` regenerates exactly these from the
published per-frame records). The bundle's `oldlight/records/logs/pretrain_evals.log` has that re-scoring's lines (its progress,
result and return-code lines) replaced by one bracketed line. Both are in `REDACTION_LEDGER.tsv` with the reason stated.

## Held, and why

On-box and not published: the desks' run logs (`RUN_LOG.md` of each desk: they quote the operator's private instructions
and carry the launch and spend bookkeeping); the second model's raw transcripts (`codex_results_r*.log`) and the scripts
that invoked it; the measurements of the January 2025 pix2pixHD discriminator (a separate desk's, and this desk's own re-scoring of that
checkpoint, `results/2024/d2025_*`, with the records of fetching it): that release remains an artefact-only release with no
performance claim, and this package does not evaluate it; the pix2pixHD desk's private gallery (found defective by the audit and superseded by the sheets
published in the bundle); the data-look desk's per-session contact and diagnostic sheets, run logs and gain, offset, mean
and standard-deviation maps (the alignment table, the per-session statistics, the homographies and the atlas are
published); the statistic desk's development scratch scripts (`geom_dev*.py`), its data-pull transcripts and the pull
script; the ARM-I desk's provider launcher and watchdog (`xcfg_lambda.py`, an operational tool) and its on-box self-test
run; the pix2pixHD checkpoints and per-frame generated outputs, which remain on the persistent filesystem of the rented
environment. The archives' `_control/` files the statistic desk copied for verification are public objects and are cited
by URL and digest (`train_free_coupling_old_sessions/CONTROL_INPUTS.md`) rather than copied again.

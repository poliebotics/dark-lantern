3. **Statistics and uncertainty: meaningful, but descriptions need fixes.** The generator test holds the real recording fixed. It compares `corr(G(E_t)−M, B_t−M)` with `corr(G(E_s)−M, B_t−M)`. Another generated image is substituted, rather than another real recording. REPORT.md:185 states this correctly; its repeated introductory explanation reverses the swap. The colour statistic averages three channel correlations, at 512×288 for 2024 and 512×320 for 2023. It is a disclosed extension to the kit’s grayscale view. Negatives are independently sampled within session, excluding self, with seed **20260909** and possible partner reuse. Sources: G implementation (`pix2pixhd_old_light/kit/eval_g_verifier.py` line 41), incorrect explanation (`pix2pixhd_old_light/REPORT.md` line 67).

   D follows the kit’s corrected v2 approach: capture swap means `D(E_t,B_s)`; emission swap means `D(E_s,B_t)`. Scores average final prediction maps across three scales. Its shared PCG64-seeded partner maps are genuine within-session bijections without self-matches. Coupling uses seed **0**, a separate mismatch draw per grid and spatial channel standardisation, without training-mean subtraction. Its shuffle repair can repeat partners, so calling that draw a “derangement” is inaccurate. Sources: D map (`pix2pixhd_old_light/kit/eval_d_verifier_v3.py` line 44), D scoring (`pix2pixhd_old_light/kit/eval_d_verifier_v3.py` line 87), coupling draw (`pix2pixhd_old_light/kit/coupling_stat.py` line 45).

   The CIs describe resampling frames with their existing mismatches. They omit temporal dependence, shared partners, negative-draw variation and model/grid selection. One session per era cannot establish uncertainty across sessions. **[1.000, 1.000] reflects perfect ordering in these sampled scores, not certainty about future performance.** Source: bootstrap implementation (`pix2pixhd_old_light/kit/ci_summary.py` line 16).

4. **Fidelity: resolution comparison PASS in the report; baseline identity and causal explanation FAIL.** The report provides matching full-model and native-resolution baselines. However, the held-session baseline and G residual mean use a deterministic sample capped at **200 training images**. For 2024, these are the first 200 sorted training frames. The supplied prior averages **all 342**. For tails, baseline means are session-specific while the prior remains global. Thus the reported constant is a valid baseline, but it is not the exact image handed to the model. Identify it accurately and add an exact supplied-prior-copy comparison before claiming improvement over that input. Sources: evaluation scales (`pix2pixhd_old_light/kit/run_evals.sh` line 9), sampled baseline (`pix2pixhd_old_light/kit/eval_baselines.py` line 33), supplied prior (`pix2pixhd_old_light/kit/mean_image.py` line 8).

   Delete **“below it, as a sharp render of an unpredictable person must be.”** Unpredictable motion can contribute error, but learning predictable illumination can still improve upon a constant. No person-mask, static-region or controlled-motion analysis attributes the measured deficit to motion. A defensible replacement is: “The generator carries emission-specific changes, while overall fidelity remains below the sampled training-mean baseline; motion-related error and model error were not separated.” Source of the unsupported claim: REPORT.md:18 (`pix2pixhd_old_light/REPORT.md` line 18).

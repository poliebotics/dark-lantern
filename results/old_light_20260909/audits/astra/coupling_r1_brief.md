Read-only. Report train_free_coupling_old_sessions/REPORT.md (every number written from results/2023_results.json and results/2024_results.json by write_report.py); code in that directory (stage_a_session.py, stage_a_extra_2024.py, stage_b_stats.py, select_2023.py, verify_*.py); run log RUN_LOG.md; bulk under [private local data]/ and, read-only, [local copies of the public 2024 HDF5 files]/. Desk headline: with an emission-blind projected-region crop (temporal standard deviation of the recordings finds the lit quadrilateral, per-side RANSAC, perspective warp), pooled AUROC matched versus one seeded same-session mismatch reaches 0.9999 to 1.000 for 2023 at grid 8 and above and 0.9988 to 1.000 for 2024 at grid 16 and above, with 584/584 and 448/448 paired wins; full-frame numbers are weaker (2023 0.74, 2024 0.58); one seeded label shuffle sits at 0.47 to 0.54 and a 1,000-permutation null band at 0.46 to 0.54; a byte-order control drops the crop result; an emission-informed refinement fitted on half the frames does not beat the blind quad on the other half; the January 2025 exporter's 75-percent-height band does not match the detected region and is reported as its own variant.
